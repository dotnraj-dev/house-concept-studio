# Stairs and terrace visual comparison

The baseline [before-ground-plan.png](before-ground-plan.png) and [before-exterior.png](before-exterior.png) were captured before edits. [after-ground-plan.png](after-ground-plan.png) and [after-exterior.png](after-exterior.png) use the same demo house, 1440 × 900 viewport, front camera, daylight and quality settings. The street-facing massing looks similar because the new roof services sit behind the front parapet; this pair is evidence of preserved composition, not a major facade improvement.

The G+1 internal case is shown in [internal-first-plan.png](internal-first-plan.png), [internal-terrace-plan.png](internal-terrace-plan.png), [internal-exterior.png](internal-exterior.png), and [internal-terrace-walk.png](internal-terrace-walk.png). The terrace plan identifies the stair headroom, roof tank, perimeter and drainage direction. The browser scene contains a deck, roof opening, cabin, door, ladder, slatted tank screen, and parapet. The Walk view lands on the terrace, but the camera is close to the cabin and the screenshot does not prove a continuous manual ascent from the ground floor.

The external side stair and independent first floor are shown in [external-ground-plan.png](external-ground-plan.png), [external-first-plan.png](external-first-plan.png), [external-terrace-plan.png](external-terrace-plan.png), [external-exterior.png](external-exterior.png), [external-side-3d.png](external-side-3d.png), and [external-terrace-walk.png](external-terrace-walk.png). [external-tank-moved.png](external-tank-moved.png) records a real B1 2D object drag; the browser proof records tank plan coordinates changing from 35.9, 61 to 27.25, 53.25 feet. The side view exposes stair flights, landings, cabin and tank. The front view screens them. The independent entrance is represented in the layout, but the external stair still has schematic rails, junctions and tread detailing; this needs construction review for safe access and weather protection.

The single-storey tiled case appears in [pitched-terrace-plan.png](pitched-terrace-plan.png), [pitched-front-3d.png](pitched-front-3d.png) and [pitched-rear-3d.png](pitched-rear-3d.png). Its front retains a tiled roof silhouette and its rear provides a flat usable terrace. The change between the pitched section and rear deck is abrupt, and the roof finish, flashing and water path need further detailing. The front capture was retaken after material loading finished; the scene rendered without a page error.

The primary comparison targets are [ext-01-contemporary-slats.png](../../references/ext-01-contemporary-slats.png), [ext-02-jaali-stone.png](../../references/ext-02-jaali-stone.png), and [ext-04-tiled-bungalow.png](../../references/ext-04-tiled-bungalow.png). The added headroom and screened tank address roof vocabulary visible in ext-01/ext-02; the pitched case keeps the dominant tile roof of ext-04. The browser work remains less convincing in proportions, material response, soffit and tile detail, planting and light. The secondary element image [detail-px-03-clay-roof-tiles.jpg](../../references/pixabay/detail-px-03-clay-roof-tiles.jpg) informs the remaining tile profile and overlap gap; it does not replace the primary reference. Functional assertions and zero page errors are recorded in [browser-proof.json](browser-proof.json) and separately from this visual judgement.
