# Realism pass: browser comparison

These are screenshots of the same editable 30 × 50 ft, G+1 demo house in the browser 3D viewer, at a 1280 × 1000 viewport. Each pair uses the Front or room jump camera, 45° Front FOV, recipe lighting, and the Auto quality *choice*. The original `before-*` files were captured before the scene changes. `before-capture.json` and `after-capture.json` retain camera positions, quality levels, renderer and browser errors. Auto changed its effective level during capture, so the pairs with different levels are directional visual evidence rather than exact pixel comparisons. No screenshot was synthesized from the primary references.

The road, car, exterior AC and facade assets are real meshes, and the room jumps are camera navigation through the same 3D house. The car's wheel arches, glazing, rims, lights and trim are modelled, but its frontal silhouette still reads simpler than a production vehicle. Transparent window glazing has a stronger environment response; the screenshot does not establish accurate reflected surroundings. Planting has more scale and rotation variation, while broad tree crowns and example neighbours remain stylized. No furniture orientation or circulation geometry was deliberately changed. Walkable doorway, stair and balcony clearances were not newly audited in this pass.

Secondary element references used to judge details: [ext-px-05-box-frame-dusk.jpg](../../references/pixabay/ext-px-05-box-frame-dusk.jpg) and [ext-px-06-modern-villa-dusk.jpg](../../references/pixabay/ext-px-06-modern-villa-dusk.jpg) for dusk exposure, and [ext-px-07-glass-railings.jpg](../../references/pixabay/ext-px-07-glass-railings.jpg) for glass response. Their provenance remains in [SOURCES.md](../../references/pixabay/SOURCES.md). Matching an element does not establish a match to the full-house primary references.

Functional results and measured fps are reported separately in [REALISM-UPDATE.md](../../../REALISM-UPDATE.md). The screenshot judgement remains partial: materials and furniture lack the primary renders' texture depth, vegetation and sky remain procedural. Auto quality measured 19.9–22.0 fps across the four Front lighting presets; the requested 40 fps target is unmet in every measured overview preset.
