# Workspace visual comparison

[Gallery](index.html) · [Full report](../../../WORKSPACE-UPDATE.md) · [Design brief](../../../AGENTS.md)

The before images were captured from the actual pre-edit application. After images are actual browser captures of the same 30×50 ft, G+1, 14-room demo project. Its legacy tn-contemporary style is unchanged; this is not a new rendering of any of the five facade recipes.

## Controlled scene pair

Compare [before-exterior.png](before-exterior.png) with [after-exterior-matched-high.png](after-exterior-matched-high.png). Both use the saved camera/FOV, 709×603 CSS canvas, default recipe daylight and High effects. The saved pose is in [before-verification.json](before-verification.json). Floating UI/compass overlays differ, and animation phases may differ; neither image is generated artwork. Whole-workspace after shots intentionally use the larger canvas and automatic quality, so they are not pixel-controlled material comparisons.

## What improved visually

- The house occupies the central available height instead of sitting below a tall project header and several tool rows.
- Brief/2D/3D/Present/Proposal, save and export have a consistent top bar; long forms and technical reviews no longer push the model down the page.
- Layers and Inspector share the same room selection, with subtle plan/model highlights and clear read-only measurements.
- Present removes application chrome while keeping the real house and customer tour controls.
- Tablet/phone panels overlay the canvas. Touch walking controls, compass, floor selector and room jump occupy separate HUD positions.

## Reference comparison and remaining differences

The style targets remain [ext-01 contemporary slats](../../references/ext-01-contemporary-slats.png), [ext-02 jaali/stone](../../references/ext-02-jaali-stone.png), [ext-03 curved villa](../../references/ext-03-curved-villa.png), [ext-04 bungalow](../../references/ext-04-tiled-bungalow.png) and [ext-05 narrow G+2](../../references/ext-05-narrow-g2.png). Interiors remain judged against int-01–04; plan legibility against plan-01–02. Pixabay images remain secondary element references.

This UI pass makes composition easier to inspect but does not improve architectural matching:

1. The unchanged legacy fixture still has its original cladding, window proportions, simplified car/plants and architectural detailing. It does not acquire ext-01's large slat plane, ext-02's jaali composition or ext-03's curves through a UI change.
2. Interior furniture, material response, lighting and shadows remain the existing scene. Enlarging a viewport does not make it photorealistic or match int-01/int-03.
3. The furnished SVG remains a measured diagram, not the rendered cutaway appearance of plan-01/02. Weak-hardware Low quality is visibly softer and drops effects.

Some walk proof images are close to the entrance because that is the existing walking spawn; room jump and drag-to-look allow interior inspection. No camera screenshot establishes improved circulation or construction readiness.

The historical per-recipe comparison remains [facade-pass-2/comparison.md](../facade-pass-2/comparison.md). No room layout, floor count, facade recipe, asset or architectural material was changed here.
