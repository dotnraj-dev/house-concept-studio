## What was researched

The reference catalogue covers all 28 Indian states. It is a set of design starting points, not a claim that an entire state has one architectural tradition. The local illustrated atlas is at exports/indian-house-atlas/index.html; its sources.json records the source, author and licence of each accepted image or drawing. Some states have photographs of interiors and exteriors; some have museum examples, an interior detail, a courtyard or a section drawing. It does not have a verified interior-and-exterior pair for every state.

The study compared actual building features: sheltered entrances, eave depth, roof silhouette, window shading, material colours, floor finishes and transitions between public and private areas. The state selection is independent of the construction location. Choosing Kerala for a Chennai project changes the visual treatment without substituting Kerala coordinates or local regulations.

Sources include:
- [DakshinaChitra: Tamil Nadu houses](https://www.dakshinachitra.net/tamilnadu)
- [Kerala Museum: vernacular architecture](https://keralamuseum.org/article/vernacular-architecture-a-connection-between-kerala-and-south-east-asia/)
- [Ministry of Rural Development / UNDP: Pahal](https://cbri.res.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/pahal.pdf)
- [Vernacular Architecture of India: regional survey](https://vernacular-architecture.in/)
- [Dronah Foundation: Karnal haveli](https://www.dronahfoundation.org/an-old-haveli-in-karnal/)
- [Madhya Pradesh Tribal Museum: Gond house](https://mptribal.museumguide.in/tribal-life/gond-house.html)
- [Khonoma: buildings and household spaces](https://khonoma.in/)

The selected preset controls a palette, roof/eaves, window shades and entrance soffit. These are inspired treatments of the current measured plan. The engine does not yet generate a complete nalukettu courtyard house, a circular bhunga, a stilted flood-resilient structure or a certified regional structural system. Individual cultural artwork is not automatically copied onto the model.

## Placement and materials

Bathroom fittings now anchor to an actual wall, avoiding the door swing envelope and window openings. The mirror shares the basin's wall. The furnished SVG and 3D use the same fixture coordinates. Crowded bathrooms may not fit every fitting; the viewer explicitly lists those bathrooms for room-size review and omits fittings that do not fit. This does not mean the underlying floor planner can satisfy every plot or room programme.

A shared model-fitting fix keeps built-in furniture in local coordinates when the viewer re-renders. Previously the parent room placement could be applied twice, shifting basins, toilets and other built-ins out of place after a control change. A regression check covers mounted and rotated models.

The obsolete front strip at waist height has been removed. It formerly crossed the entrance. Kitchen cabinets sit clear of the worktop. Upper wall heights now meet the floor soffit. The tour enters rooms from a doorway instead of starting among their furniture. A room picker gives a direct eye-level view.

Walls use a restrained painted finish rather than the overly coarse plaster texture seen in the screenshots. New original repeatable paint, limestone, asphalt, lawn and clay-roof maps are generated by scripts/make-site-materials.py. Original bundled Poly Haven materials retain their CC0 attribution. Neighbouring buildings have openings, shades and roof forms; original palm geometry replaces bright low-poly palms.

## Sun and breeze

The sun study uses editable site coordinates, a date and IST time. Its solar direction uses [NOAA's fractional-year approximation](https://www.gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/solareqns.PDF); it updates the real scene light and shadows, including the displayed neighbouring buildings. Chennai is explicitly labelled as the starting reference location. It is not a measured daylight-factor, lux, heat-gain or annual energy calculation.

The breeze overlay traces possible connections from windward to leeward windows through rooms and open doors or arches on the selected floor. Closed internal doors break the route. Window-open and direction controls are assumptions. The moving markers show route direction, not air speed. This is a qualitative opening-connectivity illustration, not CFD. [US Department of Energy guidance](https://bsesc.energy.gov/energy-basics/natural-ventilation-and-cooling) explains why operable openings and prevailing breezes matter.

## Surroundings

For every survey boundary the designer can choose open land, trees, a neighbouring house/building or a road, then edit its offset, dimensions, height and position along that edge. Coordinates and observations persist with the customer project. Illustrative defaults are marked in the editor. A neighbouring footprint that crosses the plot is excluded and flagged in the editor.

The view is constructed from the entered observations. It does not automatically retrieve satellite imagery, surveyed terrain or actual neighbouring facades. Irregular plots use their actual boundary geometry.

## Current quality

These changes improve placement, finishes and presentation while preserving a real editable 3D scene. The browser remains a real-time concept visualizer; it is not yet at the photorealistic reference-image quality requested. High-detail assets, more architectural forms and lighting art direction remain necessary for that standard. AI and paid GPU services were not used for this update.
